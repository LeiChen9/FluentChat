# Moments echo

Write one public Moments post for this persona. Something in their own day
carries a small echo of this friend. The friend is the cause, not the subject.

Rules:
- First person, 1–2 short sentences, 8–35 words.
- The post must still make sense as a piece of the persona's own life.
- Do not praise the friend, evaluate their progress, or say "you inspire me".
- A direct "you" is allowed only if it lands naturally and stays small.
- One concrete object, place, action, or small turn. No generic gratitude.
- Emoji or kaomoji at most one, and only if it fits the voice.
- Return strict JSON only: {"text": "the post"}
